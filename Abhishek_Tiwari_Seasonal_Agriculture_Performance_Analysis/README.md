## Key findings
- Kharif: average profit ≈ ₹1.79 lakh/farm; profitable farms ≈ 57.8%.
- Rabi: average profit ≈ ₹0.88 lakh/farm; profitable farms ≈ 48.9%.
- Zaid: average profit ≈ −₹0.25 lakh/farm; profitable farms ≈ 35.5%.
- Drip irrigation: average yield ≈ 6.67 t/ha and average profit ≈ ₹2.20 lakh/farm.
- Sugarcane has the highest average profit in the dataset, but the scale of its yield is much larger than other crops and should be considered when comparing crops.
